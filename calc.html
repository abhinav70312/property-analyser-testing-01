EMI + ROI Calculators

Two tools: (1) EMI — your monthly loan payment for a plot or house. (2) ROI — the profit if you buy now and sell later, including rental income.

Home loan EMI

Loan amount (Rs) — money borrowed from the bank, after your down payment.

Interest rate (% per year) — usually 8.5–9.5% for plot/home loans in 2026.

Tenure (years) — loan duration. Longer tenure = smaller EMI but more total interest.

Tip: keep EMI below 40% of monthly income.

Investment return (ROI)

Buy price (Rs) — what you pay for the property today.

Expected sell price (Rs) — what you expect after some years (use the Predict page).

Holding period (years) — how long you will own it.

Monthly rent (Rs) — income if rented out, else 0.

Result shows total profit plus the yearly growth rate of your money.
